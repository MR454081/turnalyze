Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AI percentage mean?

The percentage estimates the proportion of qualifying text likely generated by AI or AI-assisted rewriting.

What is qualifying text?

Long-form academic writing such as reports, dissertations, essays and articles. Lists, references and tables may not be included.
